Chapter 01 · First Light

Every observation begins
with your own eyes.每一次观测，都从自己的双眼开始。

You have just joined the astronomy club. Tonight, before any instrument, you learn how the eye itself sees: field of view, brightness, and the slow patience of dark adaptation. 你刚刚加入天文俱乐部。今晚，在使用任何仪器之前，先学会眼睛本身如何观看——视野、亮度，以及暗适应所需的耐心。

Field of View · 视野≈ 120°The widest view you will ever have. 你所拥有的最广视野。
Limiting Magnitude · 极限星等~ mag 6Fainter objects stay invisible. 更暗的天体无法看见。
Dark Adaptation · 暗适应~ 30 minPatience makes stars appear. 耐心让星星显现。

The naked eye sees wide — but dim nebulae and small planetary disks remain out of reach. That limit is where this story begins. 肉眼看得很广，却看不清暗淡的星云与细小的行星圆面。这个极限，正是整个故事的起点。

Chapter 02 · The Refractor

A telescope is not a single object.望远镜不是一个单独的物体，而是一套需要精确对齐的光学系统。

It is a system of carefully aligned parts. In Starlight Observatory you enter the tube blueprint and install every component yourself — in the right order. 在《星光天文台》中，你可以进入镜筒蓝图，按正确顺序亲手安装每一个内部零件。

01 / 07

Tripod三脚架

Stability first. Every arcsecond of shake is magnified with the image. 稳定是第一位的——任何抖动都会随影像一起被放大。

02 / 07

Mount支架

Two axes of motion: azimuth and altitude. It carries the telescope and lets you aim. 方位与俯仰两个转轴，承载镜筒并让你指向目标。

03 / 07

Optical Tube镜筒

A dark, straight corridor for light — and a blueprint you can open to install parts inside. 一条为光准备的暗直通道，也是一张可以打开安装内部零件的蓝图。

04 / 07

Objective Lens物镜

The front glass that gathers starlight and bends it toward a single point. 位于最前端的透镜，收集星光并将其弯折汇聚。

05 / 07

Focuser调焦装置

A rack and pinion that slides the eyepiece until light converges exactly. 齿轮齿条推动目镜移动，直到光线恰好汇聚。

06 / 07

Eyepiece目镜

A small lens that magnifies the focal-plane image for your eye. Swap it to change power. 放大焦平面影像的小透镜，更换目镜即可改变倍率。

07 / 07

Finder Scope寻星镜

A low-power guide riding on the tube. Wide enough to catch the target, aligned to hand it over. 骑在镜筒上的低倍向导，先找到目标，再交给主镜。

Chapter 03 · Optics

Focus is not a button.调焦不是简单的按钮操作，而是让光线在焦平面重新汇聚。

It is the meeting point of light. Watch parallel starlight enter the tube, bend through the objective, and converge on the focal plane. 看平行的星光进入镜筒，经过物镜弯折，最终在焦平面上重新相遇。

ƒFocal Length · 焦距The distance from lens to focus. Longer ƒ, higher power. 决定放大潜力的基础量。
×Magnification · 放大率ƒobjective ÷ ƒeyepiece. More is not always better. 由两个焦距之比决定。
°Field of View · 视野Higher power, narrower view. 倍率与视野此消彼长。
ΔSharpness · 清晰度Aperture, focus and air decide the detail. 失焦即重影。

Chapter 04 · The Limit of Glass

When the limits of glass
become visible.当玻璃镜片的极限显现，另一条道路随之开启。

Push the refractor to high power and a flaw appears: glass bends each color differently. Red and blue refuse to meet at the same focus. 将折射镜推向高倍，一个缺陷显现出来：玻璃对不同颜色的光弯折程度不同，红与蓝无法汇聚在同一个焦点上。

Enter the Newtonian · 牛顿反射式望远镜

A curved mirror at the bottom. A flat mirror at an angle.
Light folds — and the fringe disappears. 筒底一面曲面主镜，中途一面斜置副镜。光路折转，色差随之消失。

Chapter 05 · At the Eyepiece

Three views. One sky.三种视角，同一片夜空。

Every observation flows through three instruments. Wide to find, narrow to see. Switch between them at any moment. 每一次观测都在三种视角之间流转：广角负责寻找，窄视野负责看清。你可以随时切换。

View 1 · FOV 120° Naked Eye · 肉眼 The whole sky at once — but Jupiter is only a bright point. 一眼望尽整片天空，但木星只是一个亮点。
View 2 · FOV 28° Finder Scope · 寻星镜 Narrower, with a crosshair. Enough to hunt the target down — still too soft to study it. 视野收窄、带有十字线，足以锁定目标，却仍看不清它。
View 3 · FOV 6° Main Telescope · 主望远镜 Cloud bands, moons, nebulae, galaxies — detail at last. 云带、卫星、星云与星系——细节终于呈现。

Chapter 06 · A Living Sky

The sky never stays still.夜空从来不会静止。

The Earth turns beneath you. Stars drift out of frame, clouds pass through, and the air itself trembles. Observing means responding. 地球在你脚下转动：星体缓缓漂出视野，云层不时掠过，大气本身也在颤动。观测，就是不断做出回应。

Earth Rotation · 地球自转Targets drift out of a narrow field in seconds at high power. 高倍下，目标数十秒内就会漂出视野。
Clouds · 云层遮挡A passing band can hide your target mid-observation. 一条流云足以在观测中途遮住目标。
Seeing · 大气视宁度Turbulent air makes the image tremble and soften. 湍动的大气让影像颤抖、变软。
Light Pollution · 光污染City glow drowns the faintest objects. 城市的辉光淹没最暗的天体。
Adapt · 随机应变Lower the power, refocus, re-aim — the conditions decide. 降倍、重调焦、重新指向，由条件说了算。
Tracking · 追踪Match the sky's motion to hold the target centered. 匹配天球运动，把目标留在视野中央。

Chapter 07 · The Instrument Path

From your eyes
to a 500-meter dish.从你的双眼，到五百米口径的巨镜。

Eight instruments. Each one earned through the story, each one changing what you can see — and how you see it. 八种观测设备。每一台都由剧情解锁，每一台都改变你能看到什么，以及如何去看。

Stage 1

Naked Eye肉眼

The widest field in astronomy — and the beginning of everything. 天文学中最广的视野，也是一切的开端。

Stage 2

Refractor折射式望远镜

Your first optics: a lens, a tube, and the Moon in detail. 你的第一套光学系统：一枚透镜、一根镜筒，和第一次看清的月面。

Stage 3

Newtonian Reflector牛顿反射式望远镜

Mirrors end chromatic aberration. High power, honest color. 反射镜终结色差——高倍观测，颜色纯净。

Stage 4

Dobsonian多布森望远镜

A huge mirror on a simple rocker box — light-bucket for faint nebulae. 简单摇箱上的大口径主镜，为暗弱星云而生的“光桶”。

Stage 5

Cassegrain卡塞格林望远镜

Light folds twice — a long focal length in a short tube. 光路折转两次，把长焦距装进短镜筒。

Stage 6

Gregorian格里高利望远镜

A concave secondary beyond focus — the image finally upright. 焦点之外的凹面副镜，让影像终于变得正立。

Stage 7

Infrared Space Telescope红外空间望远镜

Above the atmosphere, heat itself becomes light you can read. 越过大气层，热本身成为可以阅读的光。

Stage 8

FAST Radio TelescopeFAST 射电望远镜

A 500-meter ear in the mountains, listening to pulsars. 群山之间五百米口径的“耳朵”，聆听脉冲星。
