Chapter 08 · How They Work原理详解

Four instruments,
four ways of catching light. 四台仪器，
四种捕捉光的方式。

The refractor taught you to bend light. Everything after it bends nothing — it reflects, folds, cools, or listens. Here is exactly how.

折射镜教你如何弯折光线。而在它之后的每一台设备都不再折射——它们反射、折转、制冷，或者聆听。原理如下。

Maya's rule of thumbMaya 的判断法则

“Ask three questions about any telescope: how does it collect light, where does the light come to a focus, and what wavelength is it built for? Answer those and you already understand the instrument.”

“面对任何一台望远镜，只问三个问题：它靠什么收集光、光在哪里成像、它为哪一段波长而生。答得出这三个，你就已经读懂了这台仪器。”

Reflector · design 01设计 01

Newtonian Reflector牛顿反射式望远镜 牛顿反射式望远镜Newtonian Reflector

Light never passes through glass. It falls the length of an open tube onto a parabolic primary mirror, which reflects it back up as a converging cone. Before that cone reaches a focus, a small flat secondary tilted at 45° folds the whole beam sideways, out through a hole in the tube wall and into the eyepiece.

Because reflection is wavelength-independent, red and blue land in exactly the same place. The colour fringing that limited your refractor is simply gone — which is why every large telescope built since 1670 uses mirrors.

光线全程不穿过玻璃。它沿着敞开的镜筒落到筒底的抛物面主镜上，被反射成一束向上收拢的光锥。在光锥尚未汇聚成焦点之前，一面倾斜 45° 的平面副镜把整束光折向侧面，从镜筒壁的开孔射出，进入目镜。

由于反射与波长无关，红光和蓝光落在完全相同的位置。困扰折射镜的色差就此彻底消失——这正是 1670 年以来所有大型望远镜都改用反射镜的原因。

The core idea: a mirror bounces every colour by the same angle, so mirrors have no chromatic aberration at all. 核心思想：镜面对所有颜色的反射角完全一致，因此反射镜根本不存在色差。

Mount · design 02设计 02

Dobsonian多布森望远镜 多布森望远镜Dobsonian

Optically a Dobsonian is a Newtonian. What changes is everything holding it up. John Dobson threw away the expensive equatorial mount and dropped the tube into a plywood rocker box: two large altitude bearings let it tip up and down, and the whole box turns on a ground board for azimuth. You aim by pushing it with your hand.

That simplicity is the point. Money not spent on the mount goes into glass, and a mirror gathers light in proportion to the square of its diameter — a 300 mm mirror collects nine times the light of a 100 mm one. This is the cheapest possible route to a genuinely big aperture, which is exactly what faint nebulae and galaxies require.

在光学上，多布森望远镜就是牛顿式。真正改变的是支撑它的一切。约翰·多布森抛弃了昂贵的赤道仪，把镜筒直接放进一只木质摇箱：两侧的大直径高度轴承让镜筒上下俯仰，整只摇箱则在底板上水平转动。指向目标，靠手推。

这种简朴正是它的精髓。省下的支架成本全部投入镜面，而镜面的集光能力与口径的平方成正比——300 毫米主镜的进光量是 100 毫米的九倍。这是通往真正大口径最廉价的路径，而观测暗弱的星云与星系，恰恰只认口径。

The core idea: simplify the mount to afford the aperture. Photons are what you are actually short of. 核心思想：简化支架，把预算换成口径。你真正缺的从来是光子。

Space · design 03设计 03

James Webb Space Telescope詹姆斯·韦伯空间望远镜 詹姆斯·韦伯空间望远镜James Webb Space Telescope

A 6.5-metre mirror cannot fit inside any rocket, so Webb's primary is built from 18 hexagonal beryllium segments plated in gold — gold because it reflects infrared far better than aluminium. The segments folded up for launch, unfolded in space, and are now held in a single continuous parabola by actuators that adjust each one to a fraction of a wavelength.

Webb observes infrared light, and infrared is heat: a warm telescope would blind itself with its own glow. That is what the five-layer, tennis-court-sized sunshield is for. Sun-facing side +85 °C, mirror side −233 °C. It works only if the Sun stays in one direction, which is why Webb orbits the L2 point 1.5 million km out, keeping Sun, Earth and Moon all behind the same shield.

The reward is what only infrared can show: light from the first galaxies, stretched by cosmic expansion out of the visible band entirely, and newborn stars still buried in dust that visible light cannot pass.

6.5 米的镜面装不进任何一枚火箭，因此韦伯的主镜由18 块六边形铍质镜片拼成，表面镀金——因为金对红外的反射率远胜铝。这些镜片发射时折叠收拢，入轨后展开，如今由致动器逐块微调，使整体保持为一个连续的抛物面，精度达到波长的几分之一。

韦伯观测的是红外线，而红外线就是热：一台温暖的望远镜会被自身的辉光致盲。这正是那面五层、网球场大小的遮阳罩的意义所在。向阳面 +85 ℃，镜面一侧 −233 ℃。但它成立的前提是太阳始终位于同一方向——所以韦伯运行在 150 万公里外的日地 L2 点，让太阳、地球与月球始终被同一面罩子挡在身后。

回报是只有红外才能呈现的景象：最初一批星系的光，因宇宙膨胀被拉伸到完全离开可见光波段；以及仍深埋在尘埃之中、可见光无法穿透的新生恒星。

The core idea: to see heat from the early universe, the telescope must first be colder than what it is looking at. 核心思想：要看见早期宇宙的热辐射，望远镜自己必须比观测目标更冷。

Radio · design 04设计 04

FAST Radio TelescopeFAST 射电望远镜 五百米口径球面射电望远镜Five-hundred-metre Aperture Spherical Telescope

Radio waves are metres long, not fractions of a micron, so a radio dish needs no polished glass — only a metal surface accurate to a few millimetres, and it needs to be enormous. FAST fills a natural karst depression in Guizhou with a 500-metre reflector made of 4,450 triangular panels hung on a steel cable net.

A sphere, though, has no single focus. So FAST cheats: 2,225 actuators beneath the net pull the cables in real time, deforming a 300-metre patch of the sphere into a true paraboloid aimed at the target. As the sky drifts, that paraboloid slides across the dish, and a feed cabin suspended on six cables 140 metres above rides along with it. Nothing here rotates; the mirror itself reshapes.

What it collects is not an image but a signal — pulsar ticks, hydrogen clouds, fast radio bursts. Radio astronomy is where observing stops being about looking and becomes about listening.

射电波的波长以米计，而非微米的零点几，因此射电望远镜不需要抛光玻璃——只需要一张精度到毫米级的金属面，而且必须极其巨大。FAST 把贵州一处天然喀斯特洼地填成了一面五百米口径的反射面，由 4450 块三角形面板挂在钢索网上构成。

但球面没有单一焦点。于是 FAST 用了个巧办法：索网下方的2225 台促动器实时牵拉钢索，把球面上一块 300 米的区域主动变形成一个指向目标的真实抛物面。随着天体漂移，这块抛物面在整张大锅上平移，而悬在 140 米高空、由六根钢索牵引的馈源舱同步跟随。这里没有任何东西在转动——是镜面自己在改变形状。

它收集的不是图像，而是信号：脉冲星的滴答、氢云的谱线、快速射电暴。射电天文正是观测从“看”变成“听”的那个节点。

The core idea: too big to move, so the dish stays still and actively re-shapes itself to aim. 核心思想：大到无法转动，于是整面锅静止不动，靠主动变形来完成指向。

01
Causality因果

Your build quality is the image quality.组装质量，就是成像质量。

Each part is scored on how cleanly you installed it. Those scores are averaged into an assembly score, and that score multiplies straight into clarity and stability. A rushed build does not show a warning — it shows a blurred, shaking, dimmer target. Fix the telescope and the sky sharpens.

每个零件都会按安装的精准程度评分，这些分数取平均得到组装分，而组装分直接乘进清晰度与稳定度。草率的组装不会弹出警告——它给你一个模糊、抖动、更暗的目标。修好望远镜，夜空才会变清楚。

02
Real optics真实光学

The formulas are the textbook ones.用的就是教科书上的公式。

Magnification is objective focal length divided by eyepiece focal length — not a stat on a card. Useful magnification is capped at roughly twice the aperture in millimetres, the same rule of thumb amateurs actually use; push past it and the game applies an empty-magnification penalty, exactly as the real sky does. The focuser is a part with its own precision, so a cheap one limits how finely you can ever reach best focus. Swap a mirror in and the same equations simply read the primary instead of the objective — the physics does not change with the family.

放大率就是物镜焦距除以目镜焦距，不是卡片上的一个数值。有效放大率上限约为口径毫米数的两倍——这正是业余天文界实际使用的经验法则；越过它，游戏会施加“空放大”惩罚，和真实夜空一致。调焦座本身是一个带精度参数的零件，廉价的调焦座会限制你所能达到的对焦精细程度。换成反射镜后，同一组方程只是把“物镜”读成“主镜”——物理不会因为镜种而改变。

magnification = ƒ_objective ÷ ƒ_eyepiece  ·  usefulLimit = aperture_mm × 2
03
Air wins大气说了算

On a bad night, no amount of aperture can buy its way past the atmosphere.视宁度糟糕的夜晚，再大的口径也买不通大气这一关。

Every target sets its own three thresholds — light, clarity, stability — and your build is measured against each. But the sky gets a veto. Poor seeing imposes an absolute magnification ceiling of about 55×, average seeing about 110×, and a larger objective does not raise it. Low targets suffer more air than high ones. Clouds cut the light you collect. Being out of focus costs clarity before anything else does.

This is deliberate. The comments in the source say so plainly: without an absolute cap, a bigger lens would let a player dodge the lesson entirely. So the answer to a turbulent night is not a bigger telescope — it is less magnification, which is the single hardest habit for a beginner to accept and the one this game refuses to let you skip.

每个目标都设定自己的三条门槛——进光量、清晰度、稳定度——你的配置逐项被衡量。但天空拥有否决权。视宁度差时会施加一道约 55 倍的绝对放大率上限，一般视宁度约 110 倍，而更大的物镜并不能把这条线抬高。低空目标要穿过比高空更厚的大气。云层削减你收到的光。而失焦，比任何因素都更早地吃掉清晰度。

这是有意为之。源码注释里写得直白：如果没有绝对上限，玩家只要换一块更大的镜片就能绕过整堂课。所以面对湍动的夜晚，答案不是更大的望远镜，而是降低倍率——这是新手最难接受的一个习惯，也正是这个游戏拒绝让你跳过的一课。

useful_limit_eff = min( useful_limit × seeing_eff , seeing_cap ) · observation_system.gd
04
Real sky真实星空

The sky above you is the actual sky.你头顶的这片天空，是真的。

43,124 catalogued stars from the HYG survey, positioned by real right ascension and declination. The game converts them the way an observatory does: Julian date to Greenwich sidereal time, to local sidereal time, to hour angle, to altitude and azimuth for your latitude. Planets can pull live ephemeris data. Choose from fourteen observing sites and the sky rotates accordingly.

The consequence is unusual for a game: a target genuinely below your horizon simply is not available. You cannot brute-force it. You wait for the season, or you travel to a site that can see it — which is exactly the constraint every real observer plans around.

来自 HYG 星表的 43,124 颗恒星，按真实赤经赤纬定位。游戏用天文台的方式换算它们：儒略日 → 格林尼治恒星时 → 地方恒星时 → 时角 → 你所在纬度的高度角与方位角。行星可以拉取实时星历。十四个观测地点任选，天空随之旋转。

由此产生一个在游戏里很少见的后果：真正落在地平线以下的目标，就是不可用。你没法硬来。你得等季节，或者换一个能看见它的地点——而这恰恰是每一位真实观测者都要围绕它做计划的约束。

05
Order matters顺序有意义

You cannot install the lens before the tripod.三脚架没装好，物镜就装不上去。

Assembly is drag-and-drop into physical slots with an enforced sequence: tripod, mount, tube, objective, eyepiece — and the finder last, because it has to be aligned against a telescope that already works. Reflector families add a nested optical-tube stage, where you fit the primary, secondary and spider inside the tube before the tube goes on the mount. Then comes collimation, as its own screen with its own sliders: bring the two mirrors onto one axis, or accept a soft image.

组装是拖放到真实的物理插槽里，并且强制顺序：三脚架、支架、镜筒、物镜、目镜——寻星镜放在最后，因为它必须对着一台已经能用的望远镜来校准。反射镜家族还多一层嵌套的镜筒装配：先把主镜、副镜与蜘蛛支架装进筒内，再把镜筒装上支架。之后是准直，独立界面、独立滑杆：把两面镜子调到同一根光轴上，否则就接受一个发软的影像。

06
Beyond visible超越可见光

Infrared and radio are modelled, not reskinned.红外与射电是真的建模，不是换皮。

The space telescope carries the four real instruments — NIRCam, NIRSpec, MIRI, FGS/NIRISS — with filters, dither patterns and a deployment order that follows the actual sequence: solar array, sunshield, primary, secondary, hinges, actuators. The Sun must stay 85° to 135° off boresight or you break the thermal envelope. Optics must reach about 40 K, and MIRI colder still. Because infrared is invisible, the image you produce is a false-colour composite — you assign wavelength bands to red, green and blue yourself, and learn why every published space-telescope image is a choice.

The radio dish is a pointing-dynamics problem instead: slew rate capped, momentum damped, aiming settled only below a threshold, and the whole instrument limited to 40° from zenith the way FAST genuinely is. You choose a band, fight interference, and hold integration time. Nothing is looked at. Everything is listened to.

空间望远镜搭载四台真实仪器——NIRCam、NIRSpec、MIRI、FGS/NIRISS——配有滤光片、抖动策略，以及遵循真实次序的展开流程：太阳能板、遮阳罩、主镜、副镜、铰链、致动器。太阳必须保持在视轴 85° 至 135° 之间，否则热控包线被破坏。光学系统需降到约 40 K，MIRI 还要更冷。由于红外不可见，你产出的图像是伪彩色合成图——由你自己把波段分配给红、绿、蓝三个通道，并由此明白：每一张公开发布的空间望远镜照片，都是一次选择。

射电望远镜则是一道指向动力学问题：转速有上限、动量会衰减、指向只有低于阈值才算稳定，整台设备的指向被限制在天顶 40° 以内——正如真实的 FAST。你要选波段、对抗干扰、维持积分时间。这里没有任何东西被“看”。一切都靠听。

false colour: nir_short → R  ·  nir_long → G  ·  miri_dust → B
07
Taught in time恰时而教

Every concept arrives the moment before you need it.每个概念都在你需要它的前一刻抵达。

Twenty-nine Concept Briefs and thirty-one skill tutorials are gated on first use, not dumped in a menu. Maya explains collimation the first time you must collimate, and never explains it again. One level exists purely to make you repeat an observation and confirm the colour fringe never leaves — titled, in the data, The Chromatic Aberration Experiment, with the note: this is physics, not error.

29 张概念卡与 31 个技能教程都按“首次使用”触发，不是堆在菜单里。Maya 在你第一次必须准直时讲准直，之后再也不重复。有一关的存在意义纯粹是让你重复一次观测，确认那道彩边永远不会消失——它在数据里的标题是色差确认实验，附注写着：这是物理，不是失误。
