# Astronomical catalogue data

## PSR B0329+54 / PSR J0332+5434

- **Use:** FAST source identity card and coordinate-driven sky locator.
- **Primary catalogue:** Australia Telescope National Facility, [ATNF Pulsar Catalogue](https://www.atnf.csiro.au/research/pulsar/psrcat/).
- **Identifier and coordinate cross-check:** Centre de Donnees astronomiques de Strasbourg, [SIMBAD: PSR B0329+54](https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=PSR+B0329%2B54).
- **Fields recorded:** B1950 and J2000 designations, ICRS/J2000 position, Galactic coordinates, source class, constellation, and catalogue distance estimate.
- **Accessed:** 2026-07-27.
- **Presentation note:** The game labels the distance as an estimate. Player-derived period, dispersion measure, S/N, pulse width, integration, and RFI rejection remain separate from catalogue identity values.
