## Jupiter Diversity Rework Assets (2026-09)

Added while reducing Jupiter from ten fixed missions to two (L5 and L47) and
rebuilding the other eight levels around different targets.

- `assets/telescope_parts/eyepiece_32mm.png` - original procedural pixel art
  generated for this project (numpy/PIL body-of-revolution shading), matched to
  the existing `eyepiece_20mm` / `eyepiece_10mm` icons. No third-party source.
- `assets/telescope_view/expansion/triton.png` - original procedural art: a
  faint three-gaussian stellar point standing in for Neptune's moon Triton as
  seen through an amateur telescope. No third-party source.
- `assets/telescope_view/expansion/venus_crescent.png` - display-tone derivative
  of `assets/telescope_view/official/venus.png` (NASA/JPL, public domain; see
  `assets/telescope_view/official/ATTRIBUTION.md`). A gamma/gain curve lifts the
  lit crescent for eyepiece-scale readability; geometry and phase are unchanged.

Scientific references used to check these targets: NASA Solar System pages for
Venus, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Neptune's moons, and NASA/ESA material on
the Pleiades and Betelgeuse.
