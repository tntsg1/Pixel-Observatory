# Andromeda Infrared Science Assets

These optimized game assets are derived from official NASA/JPL-Caltech science
imagery. They are used as observational data views, not as decorative concept
art. The source images were cropped and resized for the 16:9 instrument display;
their astronomical structures were not redrawn.

## Files

### `andromeda_infrared_observation.jpg`

- Source: NASA/JPL-Caltech, Spitzer Space Telescope, PIA26276
- Title: "The Infrared Face of the Andromeda Galaxy"
- Source page:
  https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/the-infrared-face-of-the-andromeda-galaxy/
- Use: exposure and processing stages

The composite separates stellar light from infrared-emitting dust and preserves
Andromeda's inclined disc, nucleus, dust rings, and asymmetric structure.
